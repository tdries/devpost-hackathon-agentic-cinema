Why this exists

Global brands ship one commercial into dozens of markets and check maybe six of them. France bans alcohol advertising under Loi Évin. Quebec bans advertising aimed at children under 13. Thailand's lèse-majesté law makes a careless royal reference a criminal matter. The UK requires every ad to pass Clearcast before it airs.

And the unregulated half costs more than the regulated half. Pepsi and Kendall Jenner. Dolce & Gabbana in China. H&M's hoodie. None of those broke a statute. Every one of them was caught by the public instead of by a process. This is the process.

What happens when you upload a video observe once, judge many

The architectural decision everything else follows from. One multimodal analyst watches the film a single time and writes down what it sees: neutral, timecoded, no verdicts. Then one adjudicator per jurisdiction judges that same fact sheet against its own rulebook, in parallel.

{# What actually happens, in one picture: who does what, what gets written to Grafana, and the one arrow that goes the other way. #}
01 Ingest Shots, audio and a transcript. The film is broken into the pieces a regulator would argue about.
02 Analyst One pass, {{ dims_total }} dimensions, and verdicts forbidden. “A woman raises a glass of red wine” is an observation. “This breaks Loi Évin” is not.
03 Adjudicators One per jurisdiction, all at once, {{ packs_total }} jurisdictions in this build, each grounding its citation against a live source. A finding is always observation × rule × citation.
04 Guard A pure rule layer with no model in it. Where a market's rule targets who appears in your ad, it refuses to auto-fix and hands you the statute.
05 Publisher Metrics, logs, annotations and dashboards, written into Grafana by the crew itself over MCP.
06 Remediator Woken by a Grafana alert. Re-letters, re-props, re-voices or regenerates the failing span, priced in euro before you press.
Two ways in: click it, or ask it agent mode

Everything above is a screen you can click. It is also a tool the agent in front of it can call, and those are the same functions, not a second implementation: an agent that answers “what is blocked in France” out of its own summary of a summary is a demo, and this one answers it by reading the store the market room reads. Ask for something nobody wrote a screen for and it writes the LogQL, builds the dashboard and hands you the URL.

1 It drives the product Start a clearance, add markets, price a fix, open a frame, search the corpus, compose a chart. {{ tools_total }} tools, every one of them a thing this console already does.
