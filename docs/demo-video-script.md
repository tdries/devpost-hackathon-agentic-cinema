Voiceover is rendered with Kokoro (voice `af_heart`, speed per beat) into `docs/voiceover/`: one WAV per beat plus `demo-voiceover.wav`, the full track with the pauses already in. Record each beat's screen action, then lay its WAV over it; the times in the table are the rendered audio lengths.

Recording setup: 1920x1080, browser at 100 percent zoom, one tab, bookmarks bar hidden.
