# Demo video script (3 minutes)

The film opens on the commercial itself, then walks the live console screen by screen. Voiceover is rendered with Kokoro (voice `af_heart`, speed per beat) into `docs/voiceover/`. `scripts/make_opening.py` builds beat 1 from the ad, `scripts/record_demo.py` drives the app and records one clip per beat, `scripts/cut_demo.py` cuts each clip to its narration and lays the track over the whole thing. Output: `docs/video/demo.mp4`.

Nothing in the recording spends: no clearance is started, no square is clicked, no agent question is sent. The finished showcase run (run_959b162a0f25, test_ad.mp4) supplies every result. The launcher and agent mode sit behind the spending door, so those two are filmed against a local instance.

Notes for a re-shoot:

- The showcase run is finished, so the market tiles do not flip and nothing wears the working ring. The rainbow ring is visible on the homepage tour door, which carries it by design. Tiles, frames and findings only sparkle while a clearance or a fix is actually in flight, which costs money to film.
- The intelligence board needs about thirty seconds for its seventeen Grafana panels to paint. The recorder waits, and the cutter drops that head.
- Archive cards load their timelapse on approach, so the recorder hovers each card before expecting it to play.
